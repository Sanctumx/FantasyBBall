# Trade finder 2-for-1 and 2-for-2 (idea 169) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (inline, per CLAUDE.md) to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add 2-for-1 trades (with the best free-agent pickup and a measured slot value) and 2-for-2 trades to the Trades finder. 1-for-1, 2x2 dA, the likelihood weights and every other tab's numbers stay exactly as they are.

**Architecture:** Per-period Win M becomes a new engine path. Plays are computed once over the whole remaining window and split into buckets per period, so `TeamWin.leagueWinByPeriod` gives one match-chance matrix per period. A new `slot_value.js` holds the week weights, the slot profile (a JSON file made by a stream_sim script) and the switches the analyst sets. `evaluateTrade` gains `aAdds`, builds the pickup-alone baseline and the 2x1 breakdown, and applies decision 2. The 2x1 search runs in two stages (plain screen of every target, then week-weighted scoring of the best 15 per give-pair). The 2x2 search screens from the one-for-one table and scores the best 400 exactly. UI work starts only after the analyst gate.

**Tech Stack:** Plain JS UMD modules (Node + browser), `node --test`, Playwright browser checks, gen.py/build.mjs.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-trade-2x1-2x2-design.md`

## The 2x1 row: formula and where the slot value enters

Notation. R0 = my roster now. R_T = R0 − {g1, g2} + {t} + {f}: the trade with pickup f. B gets g1 and g2, loses t, then drops its worst by valueZ (existing `capDrops`). R_F = R0 + {f}, then my own cap drop: the pickup alone. K = the periods left: the current one at the share still to play, then the regular season, then the playoff periods. φ_k = the share of period k left, as `weeksLeft` counts it, so a 14-day period counts as 1. u_k = the analyst's weight curve. ω_k = φ_k·u_k / Σ_j φ_j·u_j. M_k(R) = my shrunk match chance in period k averaged over the 13 other teams (`leagueWinByPeriod`). M̄(R) = Σ_k ω_k·M_k(R).

Approach 2 (chosen):
- Player part P = 100·[M̄(R_T) − M̄(R_F)]
- Pickup's own gain G = 100·[M̄(R_F) − M̄(R0)]
- Slot term S = Σ_k ω_k·s(w_k) when the trade frees one of my non-IL slots and a pickup exists, else 0. s comes from `src/slot_profile.json` (Win M points per matchup).
- **Displayed dA = P + S + G** ("of which G from the pickup"; S in its own "slot value" column)
- **Sort key = P + S when `SlotValue.SLOT_IN_SORT`** (false until the analyst validates), **else P**. The minDA filter reads the sort key.
- dB and the likelihood stay plain rest-of-season (decision 2 changes gapB only).

Approach 1 (fallback, `SlotValue.APPROACH = 1`): P and G use today's plain `rosMeans` (regular season only). S is unchanged.

P + G telescopes to the whole trade's change. S is the same for every 2x1 row of one ctx (league-wide profile), so it never reorders 2x1 rows among themselves. It only moves 2x1 rows against 1x1 and 2x2 rows in a mixed list, and shifts where the dA filter cuts.

## Engine facts this plan rests on (measured on the fixture, Node, 2026-10-29)

- **`rosMeans` does not work per period.** It calls `TeamWin.leagueWin`, which builds one forecast per team over `Team.window` (now to the end of `L.lastRegular`) with games per average week = played / `weeksLeft` (fixture 14.885), then makes one `pairKit` pass. There is no per-period loop and playoffs are excluded.
- **The naive alternative (one shallow ctx view per period) is slow and wrong.** It costs 5.83 ms per trade over 20 periods against 0.35 ms plain, and the first pass takes 2.9 s because `engCtx` is rebuilt per view. It is also wrong for IL players and drops: `ilReturn` and the drop leave times would use each period's own window, so an IL player would be "out" at the start of every period.
- **Cost of the planned path.** `pairMatch` takes 4.5 µs and `teamVec` for 13 players 8.9 µs. With only my row needed (dB stays plain), that is about 80 µs per period, so about 1.8 ms per weighted trade over 20 periods, about 5 times a plain trade.
- **Why the 2x1 search has two stages.** Weighting all 12,870 "any" trades would take about 23 s. Two stages: plain on all of them (12,870 × 0.35 ms ≈ 4.5 s), plus 780 pickup runs (≈ 0.3 s), plus the weighted top 15 per pair (1,170 × 1.8 ms ≈ 2.1 s). That is about 7 s in 78 background chunks of about 90 ms each. One give (12 pairs) takes about 1.1 s. Fewer periods remain later in the season, so it gets cheaper.
- **Fixture.** At Oct 21: 20 periods left (15 regular plus 5 playoff; periods 7 and 17-20 are 14 days long). My roster has 13 players (12 non-IL, limit 12). 412 free agents, 0 on waivers, 165 targets. `scoreLeague` takes 50 ms per season run, `draft` 0.7 s. Four seasons are in `bt_daily.json`.

## Global Constraints

- 1-for-1 and 2x2 dA, the likelihood weights (`TRADE_W`), and League/Team/Rankings numbers do not change. `src/tests/fixtures/trade_candidates_golden.json` stays equal (`trade_speed.test.js`).
- Time weighting and the slot value apply only to uneven trades (2x1). 2x2 uses the plain average and has no slot term.
- No 1-for-2, no counter-offers, no server pre-compute, no Web Worker pool.
- Decision 1: dA shows the total with "of which X from the pickup"; sort and filter use dA minus the pickup alone.
- Decision 2: the other team's likelihood subtracts the value of the player it must drop. This applies to uneven trades only, Builder trades included. 1-for-1 is unchanged.
- Decision 3: waiver-wire players are in the pool and carry a small "waiver" label on the pickup name, nothing more.
- Likelihood is secondary: the 2x2 screen ranks by estimated dA, the likelihood floor is low, and likelihood is shown but does not drive the order.
- Trade type is chosen with checkboxes (1-for-1, 2-for-1, 2-for-2, any mix). Candidates are cached per type. Sell High and Buy Low stay 1-for-1.
- No draft backtest (nothing the Model pick sees changes). The analyst designs and judges every measurement gate. Edits only through Edit/Write. Built pages are committed after `node .claude/scripts/build.mjs`. IDEAS, archive and handoff records go through the ledger worktree, never this branch.
- Results the analyst must read are named `slotprof_*`, `slotspread_*`, `trade_recall_*`, `trade_cmp_*` (the `bt_*_*.json` names are read-blocked).

## Review Focus

1. A give-pair that includes my IL player frees no non-IL slot: no pickup, S = 0, and the row is still valid (test in Task 8).
2. An empty free-agent pool, or every shortlisted free agent in `ctx.out`: no pickup, S = 0, no throw (test in Task 9).
3. No periods left (now after the last period), or playoffs only (plain `rosMeans` null): an empty list and a message, no NaN rows (test in Task 9).
4. A Builder trade loaded from a 2x1 row that the user edits so it no longer frees a slot: the pickup chip is cleared and numbers go back to plain (test in Task 15).
5. Saved v5 candidates after the upgrade are ignored, never misread as uneven rows (test in Task 13).
