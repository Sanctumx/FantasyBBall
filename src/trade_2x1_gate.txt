A. SLOT MEASUREMENT (stream_sim opts.slotArm, Task 5-6)
Arms. The treated team is one seat t. Before week `from` every arm is the default (core top 10 + 2 stream slots, 4 adds). From week `from`
the treated team drops its 10th player and runs one of these arms (all other teams are unchanged):
  STATIC_P  core top 9 + static pickup + 2 stream slots, 4 adds. The static pickup = best FA not held at week `from`, ranked by the draft
            world's projected value (backtest.setup with the arm-23 cfg), like the app's valueZ. It is held every week from then on and
            added to `held` so nobody else streams it. It costs no add (analyst call).
  STATIC_H  as STATIC_P, but ranked by stream_sim's hindsight `val`. Diagnostic only: it flatters the static arm, so s comes out low.
  S4        core top 9 + 3 stream slots, 4 adds (the league cap, as stream_sim assumes; analyst call, change if the league's cap differs).
            stream_sim's own plan rule: h0 fills slots 0,1,2, h1 refills slot 0, and slots 1-2 keep their h0 streamer.
  S6        as S4, but the treated team gets 6 adds with half-week periods kept (all 3 slots rotate). This is the upper bound for "a free
            slot also frees more adds".
  Primary contrast: S4 - STATIC_P. If the projected ranking cannot be had in a few lines, use S4 - STATIC_H and label s "conservative".
  Answer to "more adds or one more spot?": with a fixed weekly cap, the slot gives one more spot, not more adds, so S4 ships.
  S6 - S4 is reported only, and it is also a validity check (V2).
from = 1, the second week. Before that the arms are identical, so week 0 must give d = 0 exactly (V1). A per-week profile does not need
several `from` values: the static pickup, ranked by season value, is the same player whatever the week.
Drafts. 4 seasons x seeds 501-550 = 200 drafts. Draft call: draft(season, 1 + (seed % 14), seed, arm-23 cfg). scoreLeague runs with
{ seed, target: true }, as bt_adds does. The treated team rotates over all 14 seats of every draft. The arms are paired: same draft, same
scoreLeague seed, and `r()` is called the same number of times in every arm.
Halves: seeds 501-525 = H1, seeds 526-550 = H2. This is the built-in fresh-seed confirmation (items 53 and 73).
Cost: per draft, 0.7 s draft + 14 seats x 4 arms x ~0.05 s = 3.5 s. 200 drafts = about 12 min serial, about 3 min with the 4 seasons in
parallel, so it runs locally. Write a PC bat only if --smoke projects more than 10 min with seasons in parallel.
Statistic.
  d(season, seed, seat, w) = weekShare(arm) - weekShare(STATIC_P), for each week w >= from, and the same over 2-week blocks
  (block starting at w = all-play majority on the summed weekTot of weeks w and w+1).
  Cell c(season, w) = mean of d over drafts x seats. Its SE comes from per-draft means across the 50 drafts (drafts are the cluster).
  Pooled values and every SE: per-draft means over the 200 drafts.
  Robust level: s_bar = 10%-trimmed mean of the 96 cells (4 seasons x weeks 1-24). The untrimmed mean is reported alongside.
  Extreme-weeks failure (the spec's failure condition): fails if |untrimmed - trimmed| > 25% of |trimmed|, or if dropping the 5 largest
  cells moves the mean below half of itself.
  Option floor: s = max(0, value) everywhere, because the user can always just keep the static pickup.
Week map: w = 7-day blocks from opening night (stream_sim's weeks).
  The fixture calendar has 25 weeks: regular = weeks 0-15 (15 periods, period 7 is 14 days), playoffs = weeks 16-24 (period 16 is 7 days,
  periods 17-20 are 14 days).
  14-day periods (7 and 17-20) use s14, measured directly on 2-week blocks at the period's first week. It is not scaled from s, because a
  2-week matchup is a different, less noisy, contest. phi counts such a period as 1, so s14 is the right per-matchup unit.
  The ratio s14/s is reported only.
Constant or profile (early vs late). Three blocks: E = weeks 1-8, M = weeks 9-15, L = weeks 16-24 (the fantasy playoffs). D = L - E,
pooled, with its SE.
  Profile only if all four hold: D - 1.96 SE > 0; D >= 0.2 points (about a quarter of the +0.88 mean dA of an accepted #163 trade); L > E
  in at least 3 of 4 seasons; the extreme-weeks check passes.
  Profile form: three step values per block (trimmed mean of each block's cells), for s and for s14. No finer smoothing: All-Star and
  NBA Cup weeks fall on different week numbers in each season and would not carry over to another calendar.
  Otherwise: constant s = s_bar, and s14 = the trimmed mean of the 2-week block cells.
Also log (for E and diagnostics): mean per-category weekTot difference by week for S4 - STATIC_P (all 11 raw columns), and extra games per
week of S4 vs STATIC_P.
Caveats. gamesIn and `val` are hindsight in every arm. Main's stream rule is the weak one (#161: a better rule is on the unmerged
inseason-pe branch, +0.56 PE), so s is probably a lower bound: re-measure once #161's rule merges. Win M in the app uses the shrink .60,
which sits above every fitted season (#150 pooled k_M .37), so the trade term P may be overstated relative to a realized s. As the spec
says, no conversion is applied.

B. WEIGHT CURVE
Family:
  u_k = delta^(t_k) for regular periods, and u_k = beta * delta^(t_k) for playoff periods;
  t_k = weeks from now to the start of period k (0 for the current period); omega_k = phi_k * u_k / sum_j phi_j * u_j.
Evidence on delta conflicts:
  - #157's frozen-roster k by lag (.79/.59/.48/.39/.30) implies delta about 0.93 a week.
  - #146's live-roster k by lag is flat (.55/.58/.57/.54), which implies delta = 1.
  - In the H2H regular season every week counts the same toward the record.
  So delta = 1.
beta (the value of a playoff matchup relative to a regular one) can only be tuned with a prize-equity simulator, which is on unmerged
inseason-pe. Method there: regress the title or prize equity on per-week match shares in the existing #161/#163 runs; beta = (coefficient
per playoff period) / (coefficient per regular week). A survival factor per playoff round (0.5 for an even team) belongs to the same
follow-up.
What can be validated here: weight arithmetic only (Task 7 tests) and how much the curve reorders lists (C).
What cannot be validated here: any prize-equity benefit of delta or beta.
Default: flat, delta = 1, beta = 1. With this default, approach 2 differs from approach 1 only in two ways: it includes the playoff
periods, and it averages per period instead of averaging the plays.
The user's "early long-term, late near-term" behaviour therefore stays unvalidated and is a follow-up row.

C. A1 vs A2 "SIGNIFICANTLY BETTER" GATE (trade_2x1_compare.js)
Setup. Fixture, my team, list "any". nowMs = the start of periods 1, 5, 9, 13, 15 and 16, with rosters frozen.
Both approaches are scored exactly on all 12,870 trades; no two-stage search here (about 30 s per date, 3 min in total).
Rank by sort key P (S is the same for every row, so it cannot reorder).
At period 16, A1 is empty (plain rosMeans is null in the playoffs). Report that as a functional fact; it is not a ranking win. Trades are
normally locked by then.
Metrics per date:
  O10 and O25 (top-10 and top-25 overlap);
  Kendall tau-b on the union of the two top-25 lists;
  top-1 identical or not;
  mean |P2 - P1| over the A2 top 25;
  count of top-25 rows whose sign of P flips.
Also report the same metrics under an illustrative curve (delta 0.93, beta 2). This is report only and does not decide anything.
"Same": at every date where both lists exist, O10 >= 8, O25 >= 20 and tau >= 0.80, and the top 1 is identical at all but at most one date.
  Then A2 cannot be better in a way the user would see, so APPROACH = 1.
"Differ": any date fails.
  This triggers a paired prize-equity screen on inseason-pe, like #163: arms "accept the best 2x1 by A1" vs "by A2" at weeks 3, 7, 11,
  1600 drafts.
  Bar: A2 - A1 PE - 1.96 SE > 0 and ahead in >= 3 of 4 seasons. Below that, APPROACH = 1.
  Expectation, stated now: #163's SE was 0.34 for whole trade rules, and A1 vs A2 decisions differ far less, so that screen will very
  likely be inconclusive.
The screen cannot run on this branch. The user's decision 2 applies: ship APPROACH = 1 as the default with A2 behind its switch, and open a
follow-up idea row for the screen.

D. RECALL (trade_recall.js, dates: period starts 1 and 9; 2x1 also at 13)
2x2: the exact enumeration (75,816 trades) is the reference: exact top 25 by dA among trades with exact likelihood >= likFloor.
  likFloor = 0.02, fixed. The user wants a low floor and likelihood stays secondary (analyst call).
  Screen top in {400, 1000, 2000, 4000}; exact scoring at 0.35 ms makes 4000 cost about 1.4 s.
  Recall = how many of the screen's exact top 25 are in the reference top 25.
  Choose the smallest `top` with recall >= 24/25 at both dates.
  Fallbacks:
    - top = 4000 at >= 20/25: ship 4000 and log the misses (estimated vs exact dA, lineup-cap cases).
    - < 20/25 at 4000: replace the screen by full enumeration in background chunks (about 27 s on "any"), if the timing allows; else
      hold 2x2.
2x1: the reference is A2 (flat curve) exact on all 12,870 "any" trades, against the two-stage search with k in {5, 10, 15, 25}; the same
  pickup rule on both sides.
  Choose the smallest k with >= 24/25 at all three dates.
  Fallbacks: k = 25 if >= 22/25 at all three dates (log it); below that, stage 1 cannot screen for A2, so set k = 25 and leave A2 off
  even if C were met.
  Under APPROACH = 1 stage 1 is exact (recall 25/25); KEEP_2X1 is still set for the switch.
  Re-check recall whenever CURVE moves off flat.
Also report the time per stage.

E. TEAM-SPECIFIC SPREAD (slot_team_spread.js, exploration only)
Delta x = the weekly per-category difference for S4 - STATIC_P from A (block E, M or L as for the fixture's date).
For each of the 14 fixture teams at periods 1 and 9:
  s_t = Win M(team + a pseudo-player whose weekly line is Delta x) - Win M(team), plain rest-of-season, 9 cats, shipped shrink.
  This is direct, with no linearization. Per-category pseudo-players give the breakdown.
Meaningful if, at both dates, (2nd highest - 2nd lowest s_t) >= max(0.2 points, 0.5 x |s_bar|).
If s_bar is 0 under the A rules, skip E and record it as moot.
