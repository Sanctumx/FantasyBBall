How the Team tab works

Everything here is a preview of your roster. Nothing is saved or sent to Fantrax; Reset roster puts back the saved team.

The roster table

Projected games left (GL)

The games the player is projected to play from now to the end of the regular season: his team's remaining games times his projected games over 82. That already covers injuries and rest, so Plays % is not applied to it.

Plays %

A measure of your team's makeup, not of the player's health. Each night only as many players as the league's active limit can start, so with more players than slots the best per-game players take the slots and the rest sit. Plays % is the share of his team's remaining games he gets a slot for: your best players are near 100%, your worst get the least. Bench players get the nights the active players leave open. IL and Reserve rows are dimmed: they show what the player would get as one more bench player, and they count for nothing until moved.

Stats and the Plays % box

Per game shows each player's projected line. Rest of season multiplies it by his projected games left. With "Adjust player stats by Plays %" checked, every player's stats and Z are also multiplied by his Plays %. Unchecked, the player rows are untouched and only the team values use Plays %.

Team average and total

These always use Plays %: a player adds his line in proportion to how often he plays. The average is per lineup slot; the total is the whole lineup per night. Reserve and IL players add nothing.

Win C, Win M and the matrix

The matrix is your previewed team against every other team in the league, one column each. A category cell is your chance to win that category, Win C is the expected number of categories won and Win M is the chance to win the match.

An average week

Each cell is one average remaining week: every player's games are his remaining Plays % games divided by the weeks left. The Trade Analyzer instead averages the real matchups still to come, so the two tabs' Win M can differ. Chances use the Matchup Analyzer's games variance and are shrunk toward 50%.

Win C and Win M per player

The change in your Win C (hundredths) and Win M (points) from having him on the team: your team minus your team with him moved to Reserve. For IL and Reserve rows it is what moving him to the bench would add.

Z and category weights

Z is the Rankings Zscore (Standard, per game). The weights are a punt lens: 1 counts a category fully, 0 leaves it out of the Z total. They only change Z and the Z order; Win C, Win M and the matrix always count every category.

Swapping players

Type part of a name in Send out (your roster) and Bring in (every other player), press Enter or click to add chips, then Apply swap. Any number can go out and in. Incoming players land on the bench. Accents and capitals don't matter.
