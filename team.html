How the Team tab works

Everything here is a preview of your roster. Nothing is saved or sent to Fantrax; Reset roster puts back the saved team.

The roster table

Projected remaining games (GL)

The games the player is projected to play from now to the end of the NBA season (not just the league's last regular week): his team's remaining games times his projected games over 82. That already covers injuries and rest, so Plays % is not applied to it.

Plays %

A measure of your team's makeup, not of the player's health. Each night only as many players as the league's active limit can start, so with more players than slots the best per-game players take the slots and the rest sit. Plays % is the share of his team's remaining games he gets a slot for: your best players are near 100%, your worst get the least. Reserve players get the nights the active players leave open. IL rows are dimmed: they show what the player would get as one more reserve player, and they count for nothing until moved.

Stats and the Plays % box

Per game shows each player's projected line. Rest of season multiplies it by his projected games left. With "Adjust player stats by Plays %" checked, every player's stats and Z are also multiplied by his Plays %. Unchecked, the player rows are untouched and only the team values use Plays %.

Moving players

Click any column header to sort inside each group (click again to reverse, a third time to clear). MPG is his projected minutes per game. Each row has a slot select (Active, Reserve, IL); you can also drag a row onto another group's header. Moves are a preview, and Plays %, the team average, Win C, Win M and the matrix all follow. Active may go over the roster limit: the header turns orange ("Active (13 / 12)"). Active is every player not in Reserve or IL; at most 10 of them play each night, so with more than 10 the extra ones sit some nights, as Plays % shows, as Plays % shows.

Team average

The last row of the table. It always uses Plays %: a player adds his line in proportion to how often he plays, per lineup slot. Its Z is the same Plays %-weighted average of the players' Z; its Win C and Win M are your average over all opponents (the Average column of the matrix). IL players add nothing.

Win C, Win M and the matrix

The matrix is your previewed team against every other team in the league, one column each. A category cell is your chance to win that category, Win C is the expected number of categories won and Win M is the chance to win the match.

How the chances are computed

The same engine as the Rankings and Draft tabs, with no shrink toward 50%. Every team in the league, yours and each opponent's, is built from its Active and Reserve players: a player counts for his remaining Plays % games (his team's schedule, the nightly lineup cap and the Reserve refilling nights), turned into a season-equivalent total. The engine then compares the team totals with its own noise, including its games-played variance. The Trade Analyzer instead averages the real matchups still to come, so the two tabs' Win M can differ.

Win C and Win M per player

What he adds to your Win C (hundredths) and Win M (points) over an average player: your team minus a made-up average player plus him. The average player is the Plays %-weighted mean line of your Active and Reserve players with your team's total games divided by your Active spots, so he is one slot's worth of your team; the incoming player's games are his own remaining games times the share of schedulable games your lineup gets. For a player already on your Active or Reserve it is what you lose if an average-line player with his games stood in his place; for IL it is what he would add. Same as Vs average in the player list.

Z and category weights

Z is the Rankings Zscore (Standard, per game). The Z button above the table swaps the stat columns for each category's weighted Z, which add up to the Z column. The weights are a punt lens: 1 counts a category fully, 0 leaves it out of the Z total. They only change Z and the Z order; Win C, Win M and the matrix always count every category.

Swapping players

Type part of a name in Send out (your roster) and Bring in (every other player), press Enter or click to add chips, then Apply swap. Any number can go out and in. Incoming players land in the reserve. Accents and capitals don't matter. Every change (a swap, a slot move, a drag) is listed under the cards as Changes with its effect on Win M; Undo last steps back one change and Clear list empties the list. The three cards under the boxes show your team now against the saved roster: Team Zscore, Win C and Win M, each with what it was and the change.

Player list

Every player, sorted by what he does to your Win M. The Change column is the real change in your Win M (points). Vs replacement: he takes the place of your own worst Active or Reserve player (this is not the replacement level of the Rankings tab). Vs average: he takes the place of a made-up average player of your team, one Active slot's worth (for your own players: an average-line player with his games stands in his place). Win M is your chance after the change. All players are listed; use Show and Search to narrow it. The roster table above always uses Vs average.

A player already on your roster shows his gain over your worst player (the worst shows 0); his Win M after is that gain on top of your current Win M, an approximation. Click a row to bring him in (your own players go to Send out), or drag him onto a roster row to swap him into that slot, or onto a group header to add him there with no one going out. Category weights only re-sort the list, by the weighted change in category chances; the numbers shown stay real.
