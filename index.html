      How the board works

      Logging picks

      Type each pick and press Enter (taken by another team) or tap Mine. The draft order puts every pick on the right team, so the Teams panel fills itself in. If you fall behind, log missed picks in any order: your own picks go to your slot and everyone else's fill the other picks in order. Tap a pick in the recent list twice to take it back; the next pick you log goes into that spot.

      Zscore

      A player's season value against an average drafted player. Each category is scaled by how far apart teams finish, measured by simulating drafts in which every team follows ADP. Categories where teams end up bunched together reward small edges more.

      −1 and −2 Zscore

      Zscore with the player's worst category, or worst two, left out, the way he looks on a team that punts them. The dropped categories show in the cell. Edge and the rank number follow whichever Zscore is shown.

      Season or per game

      Season counts each player's projected games, so a player who misses time is worth less. Per game gives everyone the same games (the drafted-pool average), so value comes only from production when he plays. This choice drives Zscore, Gain and Edge.

      Gain

      How much a player raises your chance of winning each category against the other teams in this draft, compared with leaving the pick to a waiver-level player. Your forecast is your picks plus a typical player at each pick you have left; every other team's is its picks plus a typical player at each of its remaining picks. Unpicked slots count as twice as uncertain as a single typical player. It changes with every pick, so a third big man adds less once you have two. Numbers are hundredths of a category win, averaged over your opponents.

      Gain M

      Gain Matchup. Instead of adding up category chances, it asks how much a player raises your chance of winning the whole matchup against each other team (more categories than he wins, ties counted half), averaged over every opponent. Numbers are percentage points. It rewards strength in categories that are close and gives little for piling onto categories you already win or have no chance in. It follows the Scoring setting: weekly swings in H2H, season totals in roto.

      Head-to-head

      In H2H each week is a fresh contest, so the board adds each category's week-to-week swing. Swing comes from game-to-game variance in each stat plus the number of games a player gets in a week. Here is each category's weekly swing relative to how far apart teams sit in a week; higher means a lead there is less reliable, so it counts for less:

      The # order

      The board's ranking for your next pick. It ranks by Gain, then checks the top ten against your following pick: if a slightly better player is likely to still be there, it moves up the one who won't be. Rows that probably won't reach your pick are dimmed.

      There at / Lasts to

      The chance a player is still available at your next pick and at the one after, given he is on the board now. They come from his ADP and how much draft position varies, which grows the later a player goes. Safe until is the last pick he has an 80% chance to reach; Gone by is the first pick where that falls to 20%. Rank range is ADP plus or minus one standard deviation.

      Edge

      Fantrax ADP minus rank on the Zscore shown, in picks. Positive means he is going later than his value says he should.

      ADP

      Fantrax ADP from the projections file. The two players without one are treated as late picks with a wide range.

      Fantrax sync

      Paste your league ID (or the whole league URL) and press Connect. The board reads your draft from Fantrax every 10 seconds, sets the team count, rounds and draft order from your league, and puts each pick on the team that made it, traded picks included. Choose your team so Gain and availability are worked out for you. You can still log picks by hand; anything Fantrax reports takes priority. A drafted player missing from the projections is added as a waiver-level player.

      Limits

      Lineup positions and games caps aren't modeled. Your draft is saved in this browser only.
